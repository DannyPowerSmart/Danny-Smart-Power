Frequently Asked Questions
📦

How do I track my order?

Check the current status of your order.

Open My Orders to see your order status and tracking progress. Your order can move through Pending, Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery and Delivered.
💳

What payment methods are available?

Learn about available payment options.

Danny Smart Power can provide the payment methods currently available during checkout, including supported online payment options and Pay on Delivery where available.
🚚

How does delivery work?

Understand delivery and pickup options.

Choose the available delivery option during checkout. Delivery charges can depend on your delivery address. Store Pickup is available where offered.
👤

How do I manage my account?

Update your profile and account information.

Open Settings and select My Profile to manage your personal information. You can also change your password from Settings.
🔔

What are notifications?

Get updates about your account and orders.

Notifications can tell you when your order status changes or when Danny Smart Power sends an important account or support update. You can control notifications from Settings → Notifications.
❌

Can I cancel my order?

Learn what to do if you need to cancel.

If you need to cancel an order, contact Danny Smart Power support as soon as possible. Cancellation may depend on the current order status.
🔌

What if a product is unavailable?

Find out what to do when a product is out of stock.

If a product is unavailable, you can check the shop again later or contact support for information about availability.
💬

How do I contact support?

Chat directly with Danny Smart Power.

Use the Chat with Support button above to contact Danny Smart Power.
